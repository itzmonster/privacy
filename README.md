# Fuel Tracker Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** April 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Fuel Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Local Data Storage

We store the following data locally on your device:

- Vehicle information (make, model, year)
- Fuel entry records (date, quantity, price, odometer reading)
- User preferences and settings
- App usage statistics

All data is stored locally on your device using an SQLite database.

### 2.2 Optional Data Collection

- If you use premium features, we may collect anonymous usage analytics.
- No personally identifiable information is collected without explicit consent.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Provide core app functionality
- Calculate fuel efficiency and statistics
- Generate reports and visualizations
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Provide backup and restore capabilities

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All personal data is stored locally on your device.
- We do not transmit your fuel tracking data to external servers.
- Your data remains under your complete control.
- Backup files are created only when you explicitly request them.
- We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services:

### 5.1 Google Mobile Ads

- Used to display advertisements in the free version
- May collect device information and usage data
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 5.2 RevenueCat (Premium Features)

- Used for in-app purchase management
- Processes payment information through Google Play
- Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 6. Data Sharing

We **do not**:

- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share your fuel tracking data with advertisers
- Transfer your data to external servers (except for backup if you explicitly choose cloud backup)

We **may** share anonymized, aggregated statistics for:

- App improvement purposes
- Research and development

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access all your data within the app
- Export your data at any time
- Delete all your data by uninstalling the app
- Opt out of analytics collection
- Request information about data processing

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through:

- App update notifications
- In-app announcements
- Email (if you have provided contact information)

## 10. Data Retention

- Data is retained locally until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Backup files are retained according to your device or cloud storage settings.
- No server-side data retention, as we do not store data on servers.

## 11. Permissions Explained

### Storage Permission

- Used for importing and exporting data
- Required for backup and restore functionality
- Allows PDF report generation

### Internet Permission

- Used for displaying ads (free version)
- Required for premium feature verification
- Optional: core functionality works offline

## 12. International Users

This app can be used worldwide. Data remains on your device regardless of your location.

## 13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices:

- Email: support@fueltracker.app
- Response time: within 48 hours

## 14. Consent

By using Fuel Tracker, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

## 15. GDPR Compliance (For EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union:

- We are the data controller of your personal data
- You have the right to request data access, correction, or deletion
- You can withdraw consent at any time
- You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

## 16. California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:

- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt out of data sales (note: we do not sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights
